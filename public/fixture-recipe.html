Ingredients

  • 8 oz fine yellow cornmeal
  • 1 ½ cups buttermilk
  • 1 lb unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F with a 10-inch cast iron skillet inside.
  2. Whisk the cornmeal, baking soda and salt together in a large bowl.
  3. Beat the eggs into the buttermilk, then fold into the dry mix until just combined.
  4. Pour into the hot skillet and bake for 25 minutes until the top springs back.
